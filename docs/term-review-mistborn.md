# Mistborn term list: review notes

**Status:** 170 terms across Era 1 and Era 2, drafted by AI and checked against the Coppermind wiki
(115 confirmed by the wiki's "Introduced In" field; the rest placed from the pages and summaries the wiki
cites). Same rules as Stormlight (`docs/term-review.md`): when unsure, the later book; "mentioned early, matters
later" uses the book where it matters; names revealed later use the book where the *name* appears.

Terms per book: The Well of Ascension 17, The Hero of Ages 24, The Alloy of Law 30, Shadows of Self 24, The Bands of Mourning 34, Mistborn: Secret History 9, The Lost Metal 32.
(Book 1, *The Final Empire*, has no terms by design: nothing can spoil the first book for someone reading it.)

## Decisions already made

- Dropped alias **Jak** from Allomancer Jak (everyday word)
- Dropped **Last Emperor**: also a well-known film title
- Dropped alias **Roughs** from The Roughs (everyday word)
- Moved **Trell** 4 → 6 (named in passing in book 4; matters from book 6, per rule 2)
- Dropped alias **Wax** from Waxillium Ladrian (everyday word)
- Dropped **Wayne**: common first name (John Wayne, Bruce Wayne)
- Dropped **Rian**: common first name
- Dropped **Shayna**: common first name
- Dropped alias **Devlin** from Devlin Airs (everyday word)
- Dropped **Donny**: common nickname
- Dropped **Mistborn: Secret History**: book title (the book-name rule covers it); "Secret History" is also Donna Tartt's novel
- Dropped **Codenames**: also a popular board game
- Dropped **Cycle**: everyday word
- Renamed **Moonlight** to "Wan ShaiLu" / "Shay-I" (bare "Moonlight" is a film title and common word)
- Narrowed **Silverlight** to "Silverlight Mercantile" (bare word is a Microsoft product)
- **Harmony** is left out: it's on the allowlist (too common a word on its own).

- **Urteau** (now: The Well of Ascension): Named in book 2 as Venture seat; becomes plot-relevant in book 3.
- **Heir of the Survivor** (now: The Well of Ascension): Vin's Survivorist title; may appear late in book 1, prominent in book 2.
- **Emperor Venture** (now: The Well of Ascension): Elend's imperial title: claimed end of book 2, used throughout book 3.

- **Hemalurgy** (now: The Hero of Ages): Spikes seen in book 1, possibly named in book 2; explained in book 3. Chose later.
- **Lerasium** (now: The Hero of Ages): The bead Elend takes is in book 2; named/explained in book 3.
- **Vin's earring** (now: The Hero of Ages): Earring exists in book 1; its spike nature is the book 3 reveal.
- **Compounding** (now: The Hero of Ages): Lord Ruler's compounding revealed book 3; term used more in Era 2.
- **storage cavern** (now: The Hero of Ages): First cache found end of book 2; network of caches is book 3.
- **Trell** (now: The Bands of Mourning): Named in AoL (Miles's last words); matters from BoM. Moved 4 → 6.
- **Paalm** (now: Shadows of Self): Wiki dates her to AoL as Lessie; names Paalm/Bleeder and the reveal are SoS.
- **Bands of Mourning** (now: The Bands of Mourning): Replica mentioned in passing in book 5; real Bands matter in book 6.
- **Telsin Ladrian** (now: The Bands of Mourning): Named as Wax's sister in Alloy of Law (4) ch.3; first appears and matters in book 6.
- **Cognitive Realm** (now: Mistborn: Secret History): Cosmere-wide term (Stormlight etc.); in Mistborn it becomes central in book 7.
- **Spiritual Realm** (now: Mistborn: Secret History): Cosmere-wide term; Mistborn usage central from book 7.
- **Trellium** (now: The Lost Metal): Metal appears unnamed in book 6; named and central in book 8. Chose later.
- **Hemalurgic earring** (now: Shadows of Self): Vin's earring is book 3; the earring Harmony gives Wax (and talks through) is book 5.
