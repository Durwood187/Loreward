# 0.3.0 term review: Realm of the Elderlings, The Empyrean, Dungeon Crawler Carl

Same process and rules as Stormlight and Mistborn: AI-drafted, checked against the best available
sources, later book when unsure, "mentioned early, matters later" uses the book where it matters, and
common names/words are **context-only** (they only blur on pages clearly about that series).

**How well-checked each series is.** This differs more than before:
- **Realm of the Elderlings:** checked against Wikipedia's character list and book pages (no wiki has an
  "Introduced In" field for this series). Thinnest for *City of Dragons* and *Blood of Dragons*.
- **The Empyrean:** the fan wiki was blocked, so it was checked against fan guides and recaps. About 20
  low-confidence minor characters; the main names are solid.
- **Dungeon Crawler Carl:** checked against per-book character lists. Book 8, *A Parade of Horribles*
  (May 2026), is in the book list but has no terms yet.

Ordinary-English false-positive test (5.6 MB): **0 hits** for all three series.

**Status (Eric, Oct 2026):** all three series are marked verified. The Empyrean was checked against
the-empyrean-series.fandom.com (each page's "appears in" list and per-book sections). That moved
Quinn, Cruth, Grady, Trager and Berwyn to Onyx Storm (their deaths or survival are Onyx Storm
spoilers); moved Lynx, Avalynn and Kiandra to Iron Flame; moved Fitzgibbons, Afendra and Asher
Sorrengail to Fourth Wing (named there, nothing to spoil); and added "Duchess of Tyrrendor" to Violet
Riorson. Hedley, Iris Drue, Dalton Sisneros, Kaveh and criterion have no wiki page and are unchecked.

**Decisions already made:** in DCC, dropped the aliases "Jinx" and "Chaco" (they'd match everywhere). In
Elderlings, split the short forms Althea, Selden, Alise, Hest and Carson into context-only terms. Fixed
nonstandard type labels.
