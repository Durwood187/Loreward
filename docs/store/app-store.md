**Description**:
Loreward keeps you spoiler-free while you read.

Pick your series and the book you're on. When you search Google or browse Reddit in Safari, Loreward blurs anything that could spoil what comes next: characters you haven't met, events you haven't reached, and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode answers, which love to summarise endings. Tap any blurred item to reveal it.

• Works in Safari on Google search and Reddit
• Blurs Google's AI Overview, AI Mode and "People also ask"
• Knows which book each name and plot event belongs to
• Popular fantasy and romantasy series, with more added regularly
• 100% private: everything runs on your phone. No account, no tracking, nothing is sent anywhere

After installing, open the app for a 1-minute setup: turn the extension on in Settings, then pick your series from Safari's Page Menu.

Loreward works inside Safari only. It can't see inside other apps, such as the Reddit or Google apps.
