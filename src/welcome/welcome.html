2 What the blur labels mean

  • Spoilers past your book Names something from a later book.
  • May spoil books after your book Talks about a later book.
  • May spoil your book Talks about the book you're reading. Could give away its ending.
  • May contain spoilers for the series An AI answer on a search about your series. Google's AI answers spoil by describing what happens, so we hold them back until you click.

Click any blurred item to reveal just that one. Nothing is ever deleted.

3 Pin it

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Loreward. From there you can switch it off for a site, or reveal everything on a page.

Everything stays on your device: no accounts, no tracking, no data sent anywhere.
