# Loreward privacy policy

*Last updated: November 2026*

Loreward is a browser extension (Chrome, and Safari on iPhone) that blurs possible book spoilers on supported websites. This policy covers both.

**What it collects:** nothing. Loreward has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no servers.
It makes no network requests of its own.

**What it stores, and where:** your settings (the series and book you chose, which sites the shield
is on for, and whether it's switched on) are saved in the browser's local extension storage (Chrome's
`chrome.storage.local`, or Safari's equivalent on iPhone), on your device only. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling Loreward deletes them.

**Page content:** on supported sites (reddit.com and Google search result pages), Loreward reads the
text of posts, comments and search results *inside your browser* to decide what to blur. That text is
checked in memory and immediately discarded. It is never stored, logged or transmitted.

**The iPhone app:** the Loreward app itself only shows setup instructions. It collects nothing and
makes no network requests.

**Third parties:** none. Loreward does not sell, share or transfer any data, because it has none.

**Changes:** if this ever changes (for example, if a paid tier adds a payment provider), this policy
will be updated before the change ships, and the change will be described here.

**Contact:** durwood.extensions@gmail.com, or open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
