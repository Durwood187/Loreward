Loreward

Read your series without getting spoiled. Loreward blurs posts and search results about books you haven’t reached yet, including Google’s AI answers.

Turn it on in Safari

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions.
  2. Tap Loreward and turn it on.
  3. Under Permissions, set All Websites (or Google and Reddit) to Allow.

Pick your book

  1. Open Safari and go to Google or Reddit.
  2. Tap the Page Menu button in the address bar (aA or the puzzle piece), then Loreward.
  3. Choose your series and the book you’re on. That’s it.

Good to know

  • Works in Safari on Google search and Reddit. It can’t see inside other apps, such as the Reddit or Google apps.
  • Tap any blurred item to reveal it.
  • 100% private: everything happens on your phone. No accounts, no tracking, nothing is sent anywhere.
